A simple website

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics, or cookies. We do not offer accounts or collect information through web forms.

When you email us

We use the information in your message to reply and handle the matter you contacted us about. Please send only the information needed for your inquiry. We do not use website inquiries for advertising.

The vendor invoice page

Invoice entries are processed in your browser. The page does not store, upload, or send your entries. Closing or reloading it clears the form. AMSpirit receives an invoice only when you choose to email it.

The PDF includes the payment details you enter. A saved PDF or browser print history remains under your control, outside the page. Store and share it carefully. Invoices you email to us may be retained as business records where needed for accounting or legal obligations.
